# Claims Ledger (`CLAIMS.md`)

Every engineering, verification, empirical, and epistemic claim in `omega-triscale-observatory` v6.8.0 is tracked below with an explicit epistemic tier, reproducible verification command, artifact path, and **explicit falsification / refutation condition (what would prove us wrong)**.

Epistemic tiers:
- `program-audited` — checked by deterministic automated tests (example-based, live loopback socket/wire-spy, raw-input differential, and seeded-random property checks) and planted-mutant audits on the current commit. These are tests of the software, not formal proofs.
- `empirical` — measured on real local hardware and recorded verbatim in `results/`.
- `indicator-only` — heuristic visual or proxy indicator; explicitly NOT a rigorous mathematical or biological proof.
- `upstream-linked` — reported by supervised companion repositories and audited there, not inside this repo.
- `open-conjecture` — overarching unsolved scientific problem; strictly NOT solved or retired.
